2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when a website is loaded in your browser. They help websites function efficiently, remember your preferences, and improve user experience. Cookies may be set by the website you are visiting (first-party cookies) or by third-party services operating on that site (third-party cookies).

3. How We Use Cookies

You can also control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers allow you to:

Please note that restricting or deleting cookies may affect the functionality of our website. For guidance, refer to your browser's help documentation:
